How unlocking works

  1. Buy Pro or Pro Plus on Payhip.
  2. Your unlock code is on your receipt page and in your receipt email.
  3. Open the app, scroll to Unlock More HOS Tools, type in your code and tap Unlock.

Your unlock is saved in the browser you used. On a new phone or after clearing your browser, enter your code again.

Quick answers

Do I need to install anything?
No. It runs in your phone or computer browser. Add it to your home screen and it works like an app.

Is it a one-time price?
Yes. No subscription.

Does it replace my ELD?
No. It is a planning tool to help you decide before you commit to a load. Your ELD and the FMCSA rules are what count on the road.
